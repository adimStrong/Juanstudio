The Ligaya Caption Playbook: 6 Rules

1
Lead with Provocation or Mystery
Provocative hooks average 5,792 eng. Mystery/ultra-short average 5,512. Vague averages 639. The emotional charge of the caption matters more than anything else.
Avoid: "ilang seconds yung video?" (639)   Do: "Sino pwede maging big guy ko jan?" (14,984)
2
Ultra-Short Mystery Captions = Viral Potential
1-word captions average 5,512 engagement. "oho" (3 letters) = 10,441 eng + 251K views. The audience clicks because they NEED to know what it means.
Winners: "oho" (10,441) | "BENG BENG" (3,717) | "ZUMBA" (2,377)
3
Always Use Direct Address: "mo/ka/ko/kita"
75% of content posts use direct address. Those posts average 4,561 eng vs 2,750 without. "Gusto MO ba", "big guy KO", "mama MO" - these create a one-on-one parasocial conversation.
4
Create Double Meanings
"Gusto mo ba ng LIGAYA?" = happiness + her name. "bubuksan mo o papasukin kita?" = literal door + suggestive. Double meanings drive comments because people debate the meaning.
5
Tie to Moments and Milestones
"Gusto mo ba ng LIGAYA this valentines day?" (3,948) used seasonal context. "Happy 30k mga luvs ko!!" (9,044) used a milestone. Timely hooks amplify engagement.
6
Never Go Meta or Self-Referential
"ilang seconds yung video?" talks about the video itself instead of creating desire. The audience doesn't care about video duration - they care about the creator. Keep the focus on the relationship, not the content format.
"ilang seconds yung video?" = 639 eng (talks about video)   "oho" = 10,441 eng (creates mystery about the creator)
