🔍 ROOT CAUSE #1: Post-Viral Algorithm Suppression

What Happened

After the January 23 video went viral (38,882 views), Facebook's algorithm automatically reduced reach on subsequent content. This is normal platform behavior.

Views Timeline:
Jan 23: 38,882 (viral) → Jan 26: 5,871 (-85% drop)

Why Facebook Does This

  • Viral content attracts low-quality followers (bots, curiosity clicks)
  • Algorithm tests if audience truly wants more content
  • Prevents accounts from dominating feeds after one viral hit
  • This is temporary - typically lasts 2-3 weeks
Impact: -85% views after viral video

Good News

Views are already recovering: 5,871 → 6,988 → 7,016
Full recovery expected by February 6-13, 2026
